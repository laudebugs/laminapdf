Northwind Housing TrustQ3 Board Report · Draft

2. Repairs backlog

Open repair jobs stood at 1,3011,248 at the end of September across the four regions. Most of the work closed this quarter was routine plumbing and joinery, which the additional contractor teams appointed in July were able to schedule within the target window.

The backlog fell by 18% over the quarter, but the oldest 10% of jobs are now older than 90 days. These are mainly damp and mould cases that need a surveyor’s visit before work can be scheduled.
