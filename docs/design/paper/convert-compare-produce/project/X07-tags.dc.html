12. Repairs backlog

2Open repair jobs stood at 1,248 at the end of September across the four regions. Most of the work closed this quarter was routine plumbing and joinery, which the additional contractor teams appointed in July were able to schedule within the target window.

3The backlog fell by 18% over the quarter, but the oldest 10% of jobs are now older than 90 days. These are mainly damp and mould cases that need a surveyor’s visit before work can be scheduled, and they sit in the older parts of our stock.

4The board is asked to note the position and to consider the proposal in section 4, which would reserve surveyor time each week for the longest-waiting cases.
