Northwind Housing TrustQ3 Board Report

1. Chair’s summary

This quarter the trust closed more repairs than in any quarter since the merger, and rent collection held steady despite the cost of living pressures our tenants describe in the latest survey.

The board’s attention is drawn to three items: the age of the oldest repair jobs (section 2), the proposal to reserve surveyor time (section 4), and the two new risks added to the register (section 5).

I thank the repairs and income teams for their work over a demanding summer.
