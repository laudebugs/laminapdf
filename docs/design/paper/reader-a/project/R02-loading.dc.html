Northwind Housing TrustQ3 Board Report

1. Chair’s summary

This report covers July to September 2026 and was prepared for the board meeting on 4 November. Each section ends with the decisions the board is asked to take.

Repairs remain the area of most concern to tenants. Section 2 sets out the backlog by region and the cases that have waited longest; section 4 proposes how surveyor time could be reserved for them.

Rent collection held steady over the quarter. Section 3 reports arrears by region and the support offered to households who asked for help.

The risk register in section 5 has been reviewed by the audit committee. Two risks have changed rating since the last report.
