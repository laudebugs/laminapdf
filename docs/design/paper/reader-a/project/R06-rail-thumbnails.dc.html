Appendix A. Data notes

Repair figures come from the job management system and are taken on the last working day of each month. Backlog counts leave out jobs cancelled by tenants and jobs waiting for a tenant to agree an appointment.

Regions follow the boundaries agreed in 2025. The West region includes the former Riverside estates, which were counted separately in earlier reports.

Rent figures in section 3 are for general needs homes only. Shared ownership and garage rents are reported to the finance committee.
