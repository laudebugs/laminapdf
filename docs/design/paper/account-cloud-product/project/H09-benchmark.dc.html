Method

  • Five reference files with open licences; their sources are listed with the data.
  • Browser viewers are driven by Playwright; desktop apps by OS automation, or a screen recording with frame timing where a reader can’t be scripted.
  • Every reader runs three times on the same machine; we publish the median and every raw run.
  • lamina desktop is measured on a signed release build, not a development build.
