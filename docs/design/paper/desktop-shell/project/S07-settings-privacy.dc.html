Privacy

lamina reads your files on this computer and never uploads them. Apart from the daily update check, nothing is sent unless you turn it on here.

Crash reports

Send crash reports The error, lamina’s version, your system and the renderer. Never file names, paths or document content. Kept for 30 days. While this is off, lamina asks once after a crash.

Usage counts

Share how often each command is used Once a day, totals per command and mode, so we build what people use. No documents, names, addresses or identifiers.
