System requirements

  • macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon or Intel
  • Windows 11, x64 or Arm (Windows 10 22H2 is untested)
  • Ubuntu 22.04 or later, or a supported Fedora release, x64 or Arm

What the app sends

  • A daily check for updates. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Crash reports and usage counts, only if you turn them on.
  • Never your files or what is in them.

Opening it the first time

macOS: open the .dmg and drag lamina to Applications; it is notarised, so it opens without a warning. Windows: SmartScreen may say the app is not commonly downloaded while this beta is new; check the publisher is [PUBLISHER NAME], then choose Run anyway. Linux: make the AppImage executable and run it, or install the .deb or .rpm with your package manager.
