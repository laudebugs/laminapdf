# Bugs

One folder per bug we diagnosed, named after its symptom. Each holds `detail.md` (what the user saw, how to reproduce it, the measurements and the cause) and `resolution.md` (the fix, the tests that prove it and what was left for later). How we work a bug from report to fix: [bug triage](../guidelines/bug-triage.md).

| Bug | Symptom | Cause | Fixed in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`selection-hides-text`](selection-hides-text/detail.md) | Selecting text in the reader hid it behind solid blocks of the selection colour. | The opaque selection tint was painted over the page's glyphs, not under the ink. | `@laminapdf/viewer` ([resolution](selection-hides-text/resolution.md)) |
| [`text-not-selectable-in-reader`](text-not-selectable-in-reader/detail.md) | In the reader, dragging the mouse across a page's text selected nothing. | The layer slots above the text covered the whole page and took every press. | `@laminapdf/viewer` ([resolution](text-not-selectable-in-reader/resolution.md)) |
| [`viewer-blurry-at-zoom`](viewer-blurry-at-zoom/detail.md) | In the reader, part of a page stayed blurry at most zoom levels; 100 % and 200 % looked fine. | The viewer and the renderer sized the page differently, one pixel apart, so the renderer refused the right-hand tile. | `@laminapdf/viewer`, `@laminapdf/render` ([resolution](viewer-blurry-at-zoom/resolution.md)) |
| [`viewer-flicker-while-zooming`](viewer-flicker-while-zooming/detail.md) | The page flashed blurry at every pause in a pinch or ctrl+wheel zoom. | Each repaint for the new zoom filled the canvas with the low-resolution pass, throwing away the sharp pixels it showed. | `@laminapdf/viewer` ([resolution](viewer-flicker-while-zooming/resolution.md)) |
| [`rtl-mark-on-wrong-letter`](rtl-mark-on-wrong-letter/detail.md) | Lamina PDF's text of an Arabic paragraph put three diacritics on the neighbouring letter instead of their own; pdf.js read it exactly. | A shaper draws a mark just before its base, and its origin can fall just inside the letter drawn before, which the position test chose. | `laminapdf-text` ([resolution](rtl-mark-on-wrong-letter/resolution.md)) |
| [`shortcut-chip-cramped-keys`](shortcut-chip-cramped-keys/detail.md) | In a macOS shortcut chip, ⌘ touched the key after it and sat below the letters. | ⌘ has almost no side bearing and the keys were one string; the font draws ⌘ and the arrows below the middle of the capitals. | `@laminapdf/ui` ([resolution](shortcut-chip-cramped-keys/resolution.md)) |
| [`focus-mode-item-name-mismatch`](focus-mode-item-name-mismatch/detail.md) | axe failed the status bar's Focus mode item in the gallery: its name left out its shortcut. | An `aria-label` named the button "Focus mode" while it showed the shortcut chip too; React Aria dropped the `aria-keyshortcuts` meant to carry the keys. | `@laminapdf/ui` ([resolution](focus-mode-item-name-mismatch/resolution.md)) |
| [`viewer-zoomed-out-pages-stay-blurry`](viewer-zoomed-out-pages-stay-blurry/detail.md) | When you zoomed out far enough that more than three pages showed, the reader drew three pages sharp and left the rest blurry for good. | The canvas pool's rounding to whole pixels read as a cap, so every page asked for one of the three detail canvases. | `@laminapdf/viewer` ([resolution](viewer-zoomed-out-pages-stay-blurry/resolution.md)) |
