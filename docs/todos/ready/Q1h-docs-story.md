## Goal

This todo gives the site a path a newcomer can follow. It rewrites the overview as "What is lamina", adds "Your first PDF", "How lamina thinks about a PDF", "Pages and coordinates" and a glossary, moves "Errors and warnings" under Concepts, and replaces the autogenerated sidebar with the order in plan §3.

## Context

- [Quality plan §3](../../plans/quality.md#3-the-docs-as-a-story) (the sidebar) and [§2.1](../../plans/quality.md#21-docs-prose-and-navigation) (what's missing).
- The [docs-writing guideline](../../guidelines/docs-writing.md) ("Tell a story", "Make it a pleasure to read").
- `apps/docs/astro.config.mjs`, `apps/docs/src/content/docs/index.mdx`, and the asides that hold the concepts today (`index.mdx`, `quick-start/{browser,node,rust,workers}.mdx`, `guides/open-edit-and-save.mdx`).
- ADR 0027 (coordinates), ADR 0026 (errors), ADR 0005 §3 (edits kept apart from the opened bytes) and ADR 0007 (save modes): the facts the concepts page explains in plain words.

## Scope

**In scope**

- `index.mdx` becomes "What is lamina": what it is, who it's for and what works today. Decide on a splash hero with "Your first PDF" and "Recipes" (plan §8 question 2).
- New pages: `start/first-pdf.mdx` (Node, five minutes, using Q1e's app), `concepts/how-lamina-thinks.mdx`, `concepts/pages-and-coordinates.mdx`, `reference/glossary.mdx` (every term in `docs-prose.toml`'s glossary list).
- Runtime pages keep one-line links where the concept asides were.
- The explicit sidebar in `astro.config.mjs`, with redirects for moved pages.

**Out of scope** (and which todo covers it)

- Guides ([Q1i](./Q1i-guides-create.md), [Q1m](./Q1m-guides-read-and-edit.md)), recipes and troubleshooting ([Q1j](./Q1j-recipes-and-troubleshooting.md)).
