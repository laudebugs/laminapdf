## Goal

Give the site a path a newcomer can follow. Rewrite the overview as "What is lamina", add "Your first PDF", "How lamina thinks about a PDF", "Pages and coordinates" and a Glossary, move "Errors and warnings" under Concepts, and replace the autogenerated sidebar with the explicit order in the plan.

## Context

- [Quality plan §3](../../plans/quality.md#3-the-docs-as-a-story) (the sidebar) and [§2.1](../../plans/quality.md#21-docs-prose-and-navigation) (what's missing, with suggested rewrites).
- `apps/docs/astro.config.mjs`, `apps/docs/src/content/docs/index.mdx`, the asides that hold the concepts today (`index.mdx`, `quick-start/browser.mdx`, `node.mdx`, `rust.mdx`, `workers.mdx`, `guides/open-edit-and-save.mdx`), the [docs writing guideline](../../guidelines/docs-writing.md).
- ADR 0027 (coordinates), ADR 0026 (errors), ADR 0005 §3 (edits kept apart from the opened bytes), ADR 0007 (save modes): the facts the concepts page explains in plain words.

## Scope

**In scope**

- `index.mdx` → "What is lamina" (what, who for, what works today; decide on a splash hero with "Your first PDF" and "Recipes", plan §8 question 2).
- New pages: `start/first-pdf.mdx` (Node, five minutes, uses Q1e's app), `concepts/how-lamina-thinks.mdx`, `concepts/pages-and-coordinates.mdx`, `reference/glossary.mdx` (every term the prose check's glossary list names).
- Move the concept asides out of the runtime pages, leaving one-line links.
- Explicit sidebar in `astro.config.mjs` (no `autogenerate`); keep old URLs working with redirects where pages move.

**Out of scope** (and which todo covers it)

- Rewriting the guides ([Q1i](./Q1i-guides-plain-english.md)), Recipes and Troubleshooting ([Q1j](./Q1j-recipes-and-troubleshooting.md)).
