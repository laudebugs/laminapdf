# Changelog: the Lamina PDF desktop app

The desktop reader for Windows and Linux. Each release is tagged `desktop-v<version>`, and its notes page at `laminapdf.com/desktop/releases/<version>` is the text the updater links to. This file carries the same account for people who read the repository. macOS follows in a later release, and the Mac App Store build is a separate install.

## 0.1.0

The first beta of the desktop app, for Windows and Linux. It opens PDFs on your own computer: nothing you open is uploaded, and the app works offline.

### What it does

- **Read** PDFs in tabs and windows, on your own computer and offline.
- **Open** a PDF the way your system does: double-click it or choose "Open with". The start window lists your recent files, and Clear list empties the list and the system's own recent files.
- **Organise pages**: insert blank pages, delete, duplicate, move, reverse, rotate, crop and resize them, and set page labels, then save the file in place.
- **Edit the document's properties**: title, authors, subject, keywords, creator, producer and dates, and remove the values you choose.
- **Update itself** once a day from the beta channel; Settings turns the check off.
- **Report crashes only if you agree**: crash reports are off until you turn them on in Settings › Privacy.

### Privacy

The app checks for updates and, if you turn it on, sends crash reports. Your documents never leave your computer. [laminapdf.com/privacy](https://laminapdf.com/privacy/) lists what each request holds.

### Systems

Windows 11 on x64 or Arm, and Ubuntu 22.04 or later or a supported Fedora, on x64 or Arm. Windows 10 22H2 is untested.
