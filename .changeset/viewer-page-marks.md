`client.pageMarks()` lists the page marks (watermarks, backgrounds, headers and footers, Bates numbers) Lamina PDF added to every page, and `client.textRuns(index)` the runs of text a page shows with their boxes, both as the open document's edits leave them. An editor reads them to manage the marks and to warn of text a new header or footer would run into.
